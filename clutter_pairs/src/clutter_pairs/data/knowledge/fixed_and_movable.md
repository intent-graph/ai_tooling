# Part of the building, or movable

The test: would it stay when the occupants move out with everything they own? If it stays, it is part of the
building; if it leaves, it is movable. When unsure, the lists below decide.

## Part of the building (never clutter, never a finding for being there)

- Structure: walls, floor, ceiling, beams, columns, stairs and their railing, steps, niches, skirting boards,
  mouldings.
- Openings: doors with their frames and handles, windows with their frames, sills and fixed shutters, built-in
  blinds, curtain rails and rods.
- Heating and services: radiators and their pipes and valves, a fireplace with its hearth, mantel and fixed grate,
  a built-in stove, vents, ducts, a water heater fixed to a wall, meters, the electrical panel.
- Electricity and light: switches, sockets, ceiling lights, wall lights, spotlights, a ceiling fan.
- Fitted furniture: fitted kitchen units with their worktop, sink, tap, built-in hob, oven and extractor hood;
  built-in wardrobes and cupboards; shelves fixed into a niche.
- Bathroom fixtures: bath, shower and its screen, toilet, basin, a fixed mirror cabinet, a towel radiator.

## Movable (clutter in a clean image)

- Furniture of every kind: sofas, armchairs, chairs, stools, tables, beds, mattresses, wardrobes and dressers that
  stand free, bookcases and shelf units that stand free.
- Appliances that stand free: a fridge or a washing machine standing alone, a microwave, a kettle, a television (on a
  stand or on a wall bracket), a fan, a heater on wheels, a vacuum cleaner.
- Lamps that plug in: floor lamps, table lamps.
- Textiles: rugs, curtains and drapes (the rail stays), cushions, throws, bedding, towels, clothes.
- Belongings: pictures and frames, mirrors that hang on a hook, clocks, books, plants and pots, dishes, toys, boxes,
  bags, tools, bins, shoes.
- In a fireplace: logs, firewood, ash, fire tools and a basket for wood.

## Ambiguous cases, ruled

- A mirror: movable when it hangs on a hook; part of the building when it is a bathroom cabinet or glued wall panel.
- A wardrobe: part of the building when it is built into a wall or a niche, with no visible sides; movable otherwise.
- A kitchen appliance: part of the building when it is set into the fitted units; movable when it stands free.
- A shelf: part of the building when it is fixed into a niche or carried by brackets as part of the fitting;
  movable when it is a unit standing on the floor.
