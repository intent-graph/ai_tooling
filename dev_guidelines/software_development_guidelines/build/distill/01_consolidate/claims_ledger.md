# Claims ledger: research of rounds 1 and 2 consolidated

One row per merged claim. Every original claim id of the 10 claim-bearing research results (round 1 slots 01 to 07, round 2 slots 09 to 11; slot 08 is the coverage check, not a claims slot) appears exactly once, either alone or inside a merged row; none is dropped. Rows are ordered by origin area (slot order), then by original id; a merged row sits at the position of its earliest origin. The ledger proposes no structure: nothing is grouped by a hierarchy, owned, judged or dropped.

Columns: `id` ledger id; `statement` the origin sentence when a row holds one claim, otherwise a sentence that states only what the merged origins state in common or in non-conflicting union; `merges` original claim ids, each traceable as `build/research/<slot>/result.md::<ID>`; `areas` origin area code (MAINT rows carry the sibling area, `boundary` tags as recorded by the origin); `level` and `stack` as recorded by the origins (listed per id when they differ); `agreement` consensus, majority or contested as recorded (MAINT rows: the maintainer decision plus the origin slot's unfetched reading of public practice); `positions` every position of every origin with its force and when it wins, labelled by origin id on merged rows, then agreement notes, merge notes, cross-references to near rows kept apart and the maintainer-versus-research notes of the coverage check section 5; `certainty` grounded, inferred or open as recorded (listed per id when they differ); `sources` register ids from `sources.md`.

Merge rule: merged only when the origins state the same normative thing; positions are never averaged, every position and every source is kept. MAINT claims (source MAINT-S01, not citable) stay separate rows, except one identical to a public claim (L292), where both ids are kept.
