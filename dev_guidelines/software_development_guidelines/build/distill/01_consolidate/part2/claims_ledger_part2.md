# Claims ledger, part 2: rounds 3 research and the generic evidence of the web guideline

Part 2 of the claims ledger. Part 1 (`../claims_ledger.md`) is read-only and holds L001 to L460; this part holds rows L1001 and up, one per merged claim, and never edits part 1. Every original claim id of the four round-3 research results (12 EVOL, 13 AUTH, 14 RECOV, 15 SECD) appears exactly once, either alone or inside a merged row; every claim of the five web-guideline research results in scope is either imported (once) or listed in the skipped table at the end with its reason. None is dropped. The ledger proposes no structure: nothing is grouped by a hierarchy, owned, judged or dropped.

Columns: `id` ledger id (L1001 to L1499); `statement` the origin sentence when a row holds one claim, otherwise a sentence that states what the merged origins state in common or in non-conflicting union; `merges` original claim ids, each traceable as `build/research/<slot>/result.md::<ID>` for the four round-3 slots, and, for web claims, as `WEB:<ID>` at `../../web_architecture_guidelines/build/research/<slot>/result.md::<ID>` (relative to `build/`); `areas` origin area code (EVOL, AUTH, RECOV, SECD; `WEB:OPS`, `WEB:ARCH`, `WEB:SEC`, `WEB:THIN`, `WEB:DCON`); `level` and `stack` as recorded by the origins (listed per id when they differ; a restated web claim shows its origin stack); `agreement` and `certainty` as recorded; `positions` every position of every origin with its force and when it wins, labelled by origin id on merged rows, then restatement notes, merge notes, and the `see also` links; `sources` register ids (REG-nnn of part 1, REG-1001 and up of part 2, see `sources_part2.md`).

Rules applied:
- A new claim is merged into an existing part-1 row never; it carries `see also Lnnn` to that row instead (`states the same normative thing` where the part-1 row says the same, `related` otherwise, `possible tension` where the two may pull apart; these are my readings, not checks of equality).
- Two part-2 claims are merged only when they state the same normative thing; every position and every source of both is kept, labelled by origin id; near pairs are kept apart and cross-linked as `see also L10nn`.
- Web claims are imported only when they hold for any stack: the origin stack is `neutral`, or the claim states a generic principle under a stack-specific wording, in which case the statement is restated stack-neutral, the origin wording is kept in `positions`, and the row says so. Web claims that are specific to the web, a framework, an engine or a tool are listed as skipped with the reason. From the identity and security result only the secrets and supply-chain claims (SEC-C50 to SEC-C58) are in scope.
- Web claims keep their original ids prefixed `WEB:` (for example WEB:OPS-C12); references to the web ledger inside origin texts are rewritten `WLnnn` (web-ledger row) so they cannot be mistaken for rows of this ledger; local claim references inside origin texts (for example `C33`) are rewritten to the full origin id.
- Source ids inside text were rewritten from slot-local tags (S05) to register ids. AUTH-S21 stands for three internal maintainer claims and is mapped to the existing maintainer source REG-181 (not citable, no tier).
