Disagreements and tensions (each grounded, positions as stated by the fetched pages):
- LANG-K27 finds 98.85% of observed coercions likely harmless and 80.42% of function executions use coercion; LANG-K23 and LANG-K24 treat `==` and implicit conversion as hazards to lint. Different claims (prevalence versus risk), but later stages must carry both.
- `== null`: LANG-K23 offers `null: always | never | ignore`, default always; LANG-K32 requires `===` "except in the cases documented below" (null case lives in the guide text, not read). Authorities differ on the null exception.
- LANG-K32 states it is no longer updated and recommends TypeScript; this bears on the plan's type-layer decision (boundary 02 TYPES) and on G2 for the guide.
- LANG-K32 forbids getters and setters; LANG-K11 documents accessors as ordinary class members: a position versus a reference, not a contradiction, but a rule "no accessors" would be that guide's position only.
- LANG-K04 says `Object.is` only for meta-programming; K05 and K04 recommend `===` by default: consistent, noted for completeness.
