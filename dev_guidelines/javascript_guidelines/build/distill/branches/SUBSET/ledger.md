# Ledger: branch SUBSET

Every claim routed to SUBSET (61) is placed exactly once. Row roles follow the claim's content and the
source restrictions: a rule an admitted source supports is a `decision`; a mechanism it rests on is a
`fact`; a weaker practice with its condition, or a style-guide position that may not be evidence, is
`acceptable-when`; a refuted or restricted belief is `rejected`. A node may hold several `decision`
rows when one topic yields several rules. Rows with claim_ids `-` carry a position or a folklore belief
that the audit (`12_dissent`) rejects or restricts and that no single claim states on its own; the claim
that grounds each is named in the statement by its source id.
