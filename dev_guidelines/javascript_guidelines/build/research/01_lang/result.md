# Slot 01_lang: language semantics and safe subset (area code LANG)

## L1 headline

## L2 claims

- Strict mode is the baseline: modules and class bodies are strict by construction, strict mode throws instead of failing silently (undeclared assignment, frozen writes, legacy octal) and gives plain-call `this` the value undefined. Agreement consensus, grounded: LANG-C01 to C04, C24, C39 (C03 and C39 single-source or consensus as listed in the table).
- Equality: use `===`; `==` runs a conversion algorithm with unpredictable results; test NaN with `Number.isNaN`; `Object.is` only for +0 and -0. Agreement consensus, grounded: LANG-C17, C18, C21; single-source: C20 (Object.is), C23 (Number(x) is not validation).
- Numbers: Number is binary64 with rounded fractions and exact integers only up to 2^53 - 1; BigInt only above that, never mixed with Number, never in JSON by default, never for secrets. Agreement consensus, grounded: LANG-C28, C30 to C34; single-source: C29.
- Immutability: `const` binds, it does not freeze; prefer `const`; never `var`; `Object.freeze` is shallow and throws only in strict mode; prefer copying methods (`toSorted`) and Temporal. Agreement consensus, grounded: LANG-C35 to C40, C42, C43; single-source: C41, C44.
- `this` and classes: `this` follows the call, arrows capture it, `#` fields give real privacy, super before this, fields use define semantics. Agreement consensus for facts, single-source for Google's positions: LANG-C05 to C11, C13 (facts), C06 to C09 and C14, C15 (single-source).
- Features to avoid: `with`, deprecated and Annex B features, wrapper objects, prototype and builtin modification, `eval` (the last two as Google's positions only). Agreement consensus for `with` and Annex B: LANG-C46 to C48; single-source Google positions: C14, C16, C49.
- Modern features to prefer: `??` over logical OR for defaults, `?.` only on really nullable values, `toSorted`, `structuredClone`, `Object.hasOwn`, stage 4 features only. Agreement single-source per feature, grounded or inferred: LANG-C50 to C55, C40, C44.
- ES modules in browser and Node: module scope, single execution, extensions required, JSON attributes, require() of ESM limits, top-level await, cycles. Agreement consensus in part, single-source for Node: LANG-C57 to C64.
- Measurement evidence (2015, restricted): coercions are common and mostly harmless; many `==` are needless; dynamic checks add to lint. Agreement single-source, grounded: LANG-C25, C26, C27, C29, C56.

Contested topics:

- LANG-C12: Marking members private by an underscore naming convention: guides disagree with one another and with # private fields.
- LANG-C19: Whether to keep == null as the one deliberate shorthand for null-or-undefined, or to forbid every ==.
- LANG-C22: Whether to forbid implicit conversion shorthands (!!x, +x, '' + x) in favour of Boolean(), Number() and String(): guides and a lint rule say yes for readability, a measurement study says implicit coercions are mostly harmless.

Gaps:

- Money and exact decimal arithmetic: no admitted source states a rule; the TC39 decimal proposal's current stage is not in the finished list.
- When to prefer immutability as design guidance: no T1 states it; only guides' positions exist.
- Full clause text of ECMA-262 for classes, Annex B lists and automatic semicolon insertion was not read.
- Automatic semicolon insertion, getters and setters, `typeof` and `instanceof` pitfalls, `Symbol`, iterators and generators, destructuring and spread have no extracted claim.

### Gaps

- **Exact decimal and money arithmetic.** Searched the register for a T1 statement (MDN Number and BigInt, ECMA-262 6.1.6, finished proposals list): none says how to represent money. The TC39 decimal proposal is not among stage 4 proposals. Left open; needs the proposal repository and a T2 pair or a position.
- **Immutability as a design preference.** MDN const only reports that style guides recommend const; no T1 or T2 pair states why to prefer immutable data. Only LANG-C34 and C35 (mechanism) and guide positions exist.
- **Spec clause bodies.** Read from the living draft: 11.2.2, 7.2.13, 7.2.14, 7.3.15, 6.1.6.1, 6.1.6.2 introduction, Annex B introduction. Not read: class definition evaluation, Annex B feature lists, `JSON.parse` (belongs to slot 02), ToNumber table.
- **Not extracted although in the slot's topic list:** automatic semicolon insertion (Google 5.11.3 says always terminate; no mechanism read), `typeof null`, `instanceof` and realms, getters and setters, destructuring, spread, generators, `Symbol`, template literals, labeled statements. No admitted source in this slot was read on them.
- **Number parsing.** `parseInt` radix advice appears only in Airbnb 22.3 (T3 position, 2013 browser rationale), which is outdated by ES5; recorded nowhere as a claim.
- **ESLint rule docs (SRC-002 to SRC-005)** were read through a digest: the exact rationale wording of eqeqeq, no-var and no-implicit-coercion beyond the quoted lines was not verified.
- **SRC-004 no-var:** the digest could not state when its autofix is unsafe; do not claim it.
