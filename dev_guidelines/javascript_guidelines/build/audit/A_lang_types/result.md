# Audit result: group A_lang_types (slots 01_lang and 02_types)

Method, applying to every row: each candidate was re-fetched by the auditor with a fetch tool
that returns a model-written digest of the page, not raw text, so every page below was read
**only through its summary**, except two PDFs (TYPES-K20, TYPES-K24) whose first pages were read
as extracted text. Standing was checked on a page other than the candidate where one could be
reached; ACM, IEEE and dblp record pages were blocked (403 or bot wall), and those checks fell
back to search-engine records, said so per row. The type-layer options (TypeScript,
JSDoc-checked JavaScript, none) were judged on the same gates; no option was favoured.

## L1 headline

Close to nine in ten candidate rows survived, 29 of the 65 survivors only restricted (36 admitted, 29 admitted restricted, 8 rejected, of 73 rows); every LANG sub-topic and most TYPES sub-topics keep an admitted T1 ground, while the TYPES option "no static types" and the choice of a schema-validation approach still have no T1; certainty: grounded on the auditor's own fetches, though every page was read through a summarizing tool, not in full.

Pinned:
- Counts per slot: 01_lang: 24 admitted, 11 admitted-restricted, 5 rejected (40 rows: 35 discovered, two of them split into two documents each, plus 3 added by the audit: LANG-K30o, LANG-K31o, LANG-K36). 02_types: 12 admitted, 18 admitted-restricted, 3 rejected (33 rows).
- Sub-topics without an admitted T1 source: 01_lang: none of the seven sub-topics; finer points without T1: decimal and money arithmetic, design guidance on when to prefer immutability. 02_types: (a) type layer "none, no static types" (only TYPES-K20, T2 restricted); (b) choosing a schema-validation approach or tool (T1 exists only for each tool's facts about itself: TYPES-K28, K30, K31, K32, restricted); (c) the TC39 type-annotations proposal text (Stage 1; T1 only for its status via TYPES-K04, restricted); (d) unsoundness of user-defined type guards (no source at all). The discoverer's gap "TypeScript 6.0/7.0 roadmap and TS 7 JSDoc changes" now has T1-restricted grounds (facts only) after tier recalibration of TYPES-K12, K13, K15.
- Candidates whose standing claim was found false: none. Found stale or incomplete, not false: LANG-K02 (superseded by the 17th edition, June 2026), TYPES-K28 (designers are named on standardschema.dev), TYPES-K33 (the PR it was "folded into", 8480, carries no rationale).

## L2 claims

Per slot:
- 01_lang: 24 admitted, 11 admitted-restricted, 5 rejected. Most consequential rejections: LANG-K02 (16th edition superseded, replaced by LANG-K36, 17th edition), LANG-K30 and LANG-K31 (V8 blog posts restating stage-4 proposals; origins LANG-K30o and LANG-K31o admitted instead), LANG-K33 (Exploring JS offline, unreadable), LANG-K34 (formalizes ES5). Every one of the seven sub-topics keeps a T1. Grounded.
- 02_types: 12 admitted, 18 admitted-restricted, 3 rejected (TYPES-K03 Stage 1 draft text, TYPES-K14 champion's 2022 announcement duplicating TYPES-K02, TYPES-K33 closed Svelte PR). Sub-topics without T1: "no static types", choice of schema-validation approach, type-annotations proposal text, type-guard unsoundness. Grounded.
- 02_types tier recalibration: the TypeScript team's release posts (TYPES-K11, K12, K13), the typescript-go repository (K15) and the validation tools' own docs (K28, K30, K31, K32) moved to T1, restricted to facts about each tool's own behaviour, under the policy's standing rule on a tool's own documentation; this conflicts with the T3 tier line "maintainers' documentation of tools concerned", and the maintainer may prefer T3. Open (policy wording).
- The JSDoc-checked option rests on T1 pages that lag the current compiler: the TypeScript handbook JSDoc pages (TYPES-K06, K07) do not mention TypeScript 7.0, and K07 still lists `@enum`, whose special handling 7.0 removed (TYPES-K13); both admitted restricted to TS up to 6.x. Grounded.
- Discoverer's tension LANG-K18 vs LANG-K19 (Temporal availability) is not a contradiction: K18 lists Safari as not yet shipped, so MDN's "limited availability" still holds. Grounded.

Patterns in what failed:
- Derivatives of stage-4 proposals by credible authors (V8 blog, champion announcements) fail G4 against the proposal repository or spec. Grounded.
- Version drift fails or restricts at G2: a superseded spec edition, a Stage 1 proposal stalled since 2023, a handbook behind the released compiler, a cheat-sheet section recommending dated Node libraries. Grounded.
- Dates shown by the TypeScript handbook footer equal the fetch day: they are build dates, not content dates, so G2 cannot rest on them. Inferred (two pages showed the fetch date).
- Vendor comparisons inside otherwise admissible tool docs (Valibot's "up to 95% smaller than Zod") are excluded by restriction (G5). Grounded.
- Two candidates were unreadable or had no locatable origin (Exploring JS taken offline; Svelte rationale existing only as one PR comment). Grounded.

### 01_lang: sub-topics without an admitted T1, and what stage 3 needs

All seven sub-topics (strict mode and modules, equality and coercion, numbers and BigInt,
immutability, `this` and classes, features to avoid, modern features to prefer) keep at least
one admitted T1. Finer points still open:
- Decimal and money arithmetic: no T1. Stage 3 needs the TC39 decimal proposal's current stage (not in the finished list) and, for practice, an MDN or spec statement on representing money; otherwise the claim is recorded as open.
- When to prefer immutability (design guidance, not mechanism): no T1 states it; MDN K09 only reports what style guides recommend. Stage 3 needs a T2 pair or must record it as a position (LANG-K32, LANG-K35, restricted).
- Spec clause bodies (Annex B, SetIntegrityLevel, class definitions, strict mode code): the fetch tool shows only the table of contents of LANG-K01 and LANG-K36. Stage 3 must read the clauses directly (multipage spec or a local copy).
- Restricted sources in this slot ground positions or dated facts only; LANG-K27 and LANG-K28 are single T2 measurements each, so claims resting on them alone are `single-source`.

### 02_types: sub-topics without an admitted T1, and what stage 3 needs

- Type layer "none, no static types": no T1, and none is likely to exist for a choice of this kind. The only admitted ground is TYPES-K20 (T2, restricted, 2017 tooling). Stage 3 needs a second independent T2 (a peer-reviewed study of static types in JavaScript, or a maintainer's published rationale from a major project) for any claim beyond "single-source"; the decision stays the maintainer's (Q1).
- Choosing a schema-validation approach or tool: T1 only for each tool's facts about itself (TYPES-K28, K30, K31, K32), T2 for JSON Schema semantics (K29). Nothing admitted compares approaches or tools; stage 3 must not derive a preference from vendor pages, and needs a peer-reviewed or OWASP-level source to say more than "validate at the boundary" (which K21, K22 ground).
- TC39 type-annotations proposal text: Stage 1, so no T1 by policy; TYPES-K04 grounds its status, TYPES-K02 its intent. Nothing more is needed unless the proposal advances.
- Unsoundness of user-defined type guards (`x is T` bodies unchecked): no source found; TYPES-K10 is silent. LANG-K26 documents a different, intentional unsoundness. Stage 3 should look in the TypeScript handbook's type-predicate and assertion-function sections or the TypeScript FAQ or design notes on soundness.
- JSDoc-checked JavaScript: T1 present but restricted to TypeScript up to 6.x (TYPES-K06, K07); for 7.0 the release notes TYPES-K13 and the archived port repository K15 hold the differences as facts. Stage 3 should re-check whether the handbook has been updated for 7.0 before extracting.
- `JSON.parse` clause (TYPES-K26): admitted but unread; stage 3 must read the clause itself.
