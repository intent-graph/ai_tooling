## Core Concept
<!-- Lines 34-59 -->

**Definition:** Team conventions are explicit agreements about how to write, organize, and collaborate on code. They reduce recurring decisions and debates.

**Key Principles:**
- Explicit beats implicit
- Automate what you can enforce
- Document what you can't automate
- Review conventions periodically

**The Tradeoff:**
- Too few conventions → Inconsistency, debates
- Too many conventions → Bureaucracy, rigidity
- Sweet spot → Cover friction points, allow judgment

**Convention Categories:**
- Formatting (automate with tools)
- Naming (document in style guide)
- Architecture (document in ADRs)
- Process (document in CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Status

<!-- Lines 277-281 -->

- ACID compliance for financial transactions
- Complex queries with joins

## Decision
<!-- Lines 284-285 -->
We will use PostgreSQL as our primary database.

## Alternatives Considered
<!-- Lines 288-290 -->
- **MongoDB**: Flexible schema, but we need strong consistency
- **MySQL**: Similar to Postgres, but team prefers Postgres

## Consequences
<!-- Lines 293-293 -->

<!-- Lines 296-299 -->

- Rich query capabilities
- Excellent tooling

### Negative
<!-- Lines 302-305 -->
