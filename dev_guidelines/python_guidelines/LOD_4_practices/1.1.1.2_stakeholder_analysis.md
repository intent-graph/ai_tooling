## Stakeholder Matrix
<!-- Lines 16-24 -->

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs | Priority |
|-------------|------|-------|----------|
| End Users | Use the system daily | Speed, simplicity | High |
| Admins | Configure and maintain | Control, visibility | High |
| Management | Oversee results | Reports, metrics | Medium |
| Support | Help users | Debugging tools | Medium |
| Developers | Build and maintain | Clear requirements | High |

## Key Questions Per Stakeholder
<!-- Lines 27-32 -->

- What problem does this solve for them?
- How will they interact with the system?
- What would success look like for them?
- What would make them reject the system?
