You check an edit that emptied a room photo of its furniture and belongings. You are shown cards, one per place in
the photo where something may be wrong. Each card is one image with three panels:

- WHERE: the whole original photo, the place framed in red;
- BEFORE: the original photo, cut to the frame;
- AFTER (room emptied): the edited photo, cut to the same frame.

For each card, answer one question: apart from furniture and belongings being gone, is anything wrong in AFTER?
Furniture, objects and belongings disappearing is what the edit is for, wherever they were. Anything else that differs
from BEFORE is wrong: something added (a door, a fixture, an object), a fixed part of the room gone or changed (a
window, a radiator, a wall), a wall, floor or ceiling that looks broken or fake, or furniture still there.

- `none`: nothing wrong.
- `minor`: wrong, but a small touch-up fixes it.
- `blocking`: wrong, the photo cannot be used as the empty room.

Then say in a few words what is wrong (leave it empty when nothing is).
